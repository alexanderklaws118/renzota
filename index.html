How it works

1. Choose an available category and opportunity.


2. Review the package, permitted amount, terms and risks.


3. Select a proposed payout date or investment duration.


4. Review the terms and submit your request.


5. Follow your request through your customer account once account services are configured.

This first version is a website demonstration. It does not accept investment funds, execute investments, calculate investment profits or process withdrawals.

Early-withdrawal policy

A 20% early-withdrawal fee may apply before the agreed payout date only where legally permitted and disclosed in the applicable agreement. No early-withdrawal fee applies for withdrawal on the agreed payout date. Investment losses, eligibility and other terms may affect the amount payable.
